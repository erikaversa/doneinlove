# Done In Love

Done In Love: Where memories live on, and love never ends.
I built this app because I know the pain of losing the people who mean everything to you. My family has seen too much—premature deaths and the long, heavy road of illness.
This is your private space to:

Preserve your voice so your family can always hear you.

Share amazing memories with the ones you cherish most.

Stay connected forever, even when you have to say goodbye.

## Development Setup
1. Copy `.env.example` to `.env`
2. Fill in your Supabase and Gemini keys
3. `python -m venv venv && source venv/bin/activate`
4. `pip install -r requirements.txt` (or equivalent)
